Detailed Description

Validates a session once, indexes it, and decodes selected records on demand.

The reader has no record-count policy limit. Normal recordings retain an immutable source snapshot plus a complete per-record location index, making high-rate random access independent of file-system seek performance. Resource limits are adaptive: oversized sources remain file-backed and indexes exceeding the resident-index budget fall back to timestamped checkpoints plus one page covering at most 4,096 records. Random-access methods remain confined to the reader's owning thread.
