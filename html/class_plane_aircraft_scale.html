double PlaneAircraftScale::metersPerSceneUnit ( float forwardExtentSceneUnits)
staticnodiscardnoexcept

Returns the metric length represented by one normalized scene unit.

The aircraft remains at scale 1 in every mode. Surface geometry uses this conversion so the normalized forward extent represents exactly F16LengthMeters.

The operation follows the contract of the owning type and preserves its documented invariants.
