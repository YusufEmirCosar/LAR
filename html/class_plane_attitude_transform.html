Converts yaw, pitch, and roll to an intrinsic aircraft rotation.

World axes are east +X, up +Y, and north -Z. Positive protocol yaw turns clockwise from north, positive pitch raises the nose, and positive roll lowers the right wing. Unavailable components remain neutral.

The operation follows the contract of the owning type and preserves its documented invariants.
